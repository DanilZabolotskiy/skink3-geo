## What each layer is made of

| Layer | Source | How |
|---|---|---|
| `maria` | LROC Global Mare Boundaries (Nelson et al. 2014), digitised from LROC WAC mosaics, IAU Moon 2000 | thinned on the sphere to 0.15° of arc; maria and islands in them under 300 km² left out |
